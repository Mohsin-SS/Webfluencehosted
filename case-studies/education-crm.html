(Case Study)

Most education consultancies don't have a lead problem.
They have a workflow problem.

Leads come through WhatsApp, social media, calls and referrals. Then come counseling, applications, documents, deadlines, follow-ups and university offers.

When all of that is managed across spreadsheets, chats and disconnected tools, things inevitably get missed.

So we built a CRM designed around the actual workflow of an education consultancy.

Lead management & pipeline tracking

Student profiles and application management

Counselor workflows

Document & deadline tracking

Management-level visibility

WhatsApp automation

Automated follow-ups and communication

(The journey)

The goal wasn't simply to build another dashboard. It was to connect the entire journey.

Student Inquiry
Qualification
Counseling
Application
Documents
Offer
Enrollment

Focus on conversations that actually require you.

And with WhatsApp automation connected to the CRM, repetitive communication can be automated while counselors remain focused on the conversations that actually matter.

This is the kind of software we enjoy building at Webfluence, systems designed around how a business actually operates, rather than forcing a business to adapt to generic software.

If you're running an education consultancy and still managing leads, applications and follow-ups across multiple disconnected tools, we'd be happy to show you what this workflow could look like for your business.
