The problem

Leads were dying in WhatsApp DMs.

Times Consultant was getting hundreds of student enquiries a month. The team was drowning in chats, and management was flying blind.

  • Students waited hours for a reply

    Every first response depended on a counselor being free. After hours, nobody answered.

  • No record of who said what

    Conversations lived on personal phones. A lost chat meant a lost student.

  • Zero visibility on counselor performance

    Who was converting? Who was ghosting students? Management had no way to know.

So we rebuilt everything.

Solution 1 of 2: the WhatsApp bot

A bot that onboards students while the team sleeps.

Lead synced to CRM

500+WhatsApp users connected straight into the CRM through the chatbot
  • Instant first reply, 24/7. No student waits for office hours again.
  • Menu-driven qualification. Country, budget and timeline are captured before a human steps in.
  • Every chat auto-logged. Each conversation becomes a lead in the CRM and is routed to a counselor.
  • Consultations booked inside the chat. No forms, no back-and-forth.

Solution 2 of 2: AI analysis

Every counselor, scored by AI.

The CRM reads every WhatsApp conversation and tells management exactly who is converting, who is slow, and who needs coaching.
