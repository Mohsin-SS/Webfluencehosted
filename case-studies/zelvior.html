01

A storefront that feels like the clothes

Heavyweight wool, satin, silk linings, brass snaps. The brand sells on construction and restraint, so the storefront needs the same: quiet layouts, large photography and a product page that settles fit first. Size buttons, a quantity stepper, Add to cart and Buy now sit above the fold, with "ships within 3 to 5 business days" stated directly beneath them.

Visit zelviorofficial.pk
02

What the customer sees rests on what they don't

A Shopify store is a theme, a set of apps, store settings and a checkout, all of which have to agree. We trace layout and behaviour problems back to the template that causes them, review how each third-party app interacts with the theme, and check variants, inventory, shipping and currency settings so a size shows as available and a delivery charge appears correctly. Every change is followed by the same test: open a collection, choose a piece, select a size, add to cart, pay.

Request a Consultation
03

A beautiful store is not enough if the payment fails

The last two steps are where an order is won or lost. Webfluence configures the payment options Zelvior offers, troubleshoots payment issues and tests the full order journey on desktop and mobile. The live checkout shows debit and credit card (Visa, Mastercard and UnionPay) and cash on delivery, the option many shoppers in Pakistan expect for a first order with a brand.

04

From a search to a product page in two clicks

The goal is a store that search engines can read and shoppers can arrive at without an ad. On the live store that means a descriptive title and meta description, a canonical URL, readable collection and product URLs, image alt text that names the piece, homepage copy that links to both collections and an FAQ answering delivery, restocks, currency and sizing in plain text. We keep working on copy for each drop, internal links and Search Console coverage.

Visit zelviorofficial.pk
05

Marketing brings her to the store. The store finishes the job.

Zelvior already has the creative: campaign film, editorial photography and an audience on Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp. We connect that to the storefront. An ad for the Juliet set lands on the Juliet set, not the homepage; creative follows the same palette and tone as the site; and campaign data shows which pieces earn attention while site data shows where shoppers stop. Both inform what we change next.
