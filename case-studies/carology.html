The Carology Case Study

AI Car Customiser

How Webfluence built an AI customiser for The Carology's car accessories store. Shoppers upload a photo of their car, pick from 715 parts and see a photorealistic preview of the part fitted before they buy.

Book a ConsultationTry the live customiser

715 parts to preview

The part picker reads The Carology's whole catalogue, so any product in the store can be tried on a customer's own car.

Previews in seconds

Shoppers upload one photo and get a photorealistic render of the part fitted, then stack more parts on top of it.

Checkout from the preview

Every catalogue part carries its store price, with an add to cart button directly under the preview.

01

Upload once, stack modifications

A shopper uploads a photo of their car one time. Each part they add builds on the previous preview, so a full build comes together one change at a time across exterior, interior and wheels.

Try the live customiser
02

Number plate visualiser

Shoppers type their registration and choose a white or yellow plate. One tap puts that exact plate on the uploaded photo, front or rear, with 3D Gel Number Plates already selected in the studio.

Try the live customiser
03

Every version saved

Each modification is saved as its own version. Tapping any version carries on from that point, so a shopper can compare two front grilles on the same car without starting over.

Request a Consultation
04

Connected to the store

Previews link to real catalogue products with their prices, like 3D Gel Number Plates at Rs. 7,300. A part described in words can be previewed too, but only catalogue products go to cart, so every order maps to something the store stocks.
