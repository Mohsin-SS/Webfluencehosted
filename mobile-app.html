Frequently asked questions

Should I build native or cross-platform?+

For most business apps, cross-platform (Flutter or React Native) delivers both platforms faster and at lower cost. We recommend native when you need heavy device integration or top-tier performance.

How long does it take to build an app?+

A focused first version (MVP) usually takes 8–14 weeks, depending on features, integrations and the backend required.

Do you publish the app to the stores?+

Yes. We prepare store listings, screenshots and builds, and manage the submission and review process for both stores.

Can you take over an existing app?+

Yes. We start with a code and architecture review, then stabilise, fix and extend the app from there.
