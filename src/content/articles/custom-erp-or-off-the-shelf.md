Choose off-the-shelf ERP software when your processes are standard and you are willing to adapt to the software. Choose a custom ERP when your way of working is part of your advantage, when ready-made tools would force awkward workarounds, or when you need the system to connect closely with the other tools you already use.

## What does an ERP actually do?

An ERP (enterprise resource planning) system keeps your core operations, such as inventory, purchasing, sales, finance and HR, in one shared system instead of separate spreadsheets and disconnected apps. Everyone works from the same live data.

## When is off-the-shelf enough?

- Your processes match common practice for your industry.
- You need to start quickly and can accept the software's way of doing things.
- Your team is small and the number of users is limited.
- You do not need deep integration with other systems.

## When is a custom ERP worth it?

- **Your process is unusual.** For example, a manufacturer with its own costing method, or a business that tracks stock in a way packaged software does not support.
- **Workarounds are piling up.** Staff keep exporting to Excel to do what the software cannot.
- **You need it to connect.** The ERP must work with your CRM, website, WhatsApp or accounting tools.
- **You want to own it.** A custom system is built around you and can grow with you.

## Questions to answer before you choose

1. Which departments must use the system on day one, and which can come later?
2. Which tasks take the most manual time today?
3. What reports do managers need, and how often?
4. Which existing tools must it connect with?
5. How many people will use it, and on which devices?

## Can you start small?

Yes, and it is usually the better approach. Start with the area that causes the most pain (often inventory or finance), get it working well, then add departments.
