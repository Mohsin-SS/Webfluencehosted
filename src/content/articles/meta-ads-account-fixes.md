Before you raise a Meta Ads budget, make sure the money is reaching the right people: ads shown only where you deliver, each product shown to the audience that buys it, clear captions, and purchase tracking that matches your store. Spending more on an account with these problems mostly buys more waste.

## 1. Are your ads reaching people outside Pakistan?

Meta's location setting can include people who were only recently in a place, and broad or Advantage+ audiences can drift wider than intended. If you only deliver in Pakistan, open each ad set and confirm the location is Pakistan (or the cities you serve) and check the *Breakdown → By delivery → Country* report for spend going elsewhere.

When Webfluence took over the Meta account for [Adoro (ECS)](/work/adoro), targeting was reaching people outside Pakistan. Fixing it was one of the first changes.

## 2. Is each product shown to the right audience?

If you sell to more than one audience, such as men's and women's ranges, each ad needs to reach the people who buy that product. Mixed audiences make the algorithm learn slowly and waste impressions. For Adoro we matched women's and men's ads to the right audiences, and men's new arrivals became a proven winning campaign.

## 3. Do your captions say what the product is and what it costs?

Captions are often written once and copied across ads. Check every live ad for the right product name, price or offer, and a clear next step. Errors here make a good creative look careless and lower the click-through rate.

## 4. Does Meta count real purchases?

As with Google Ads, Meta can only optimise for what it can see. Confirm the Meta pixel (and, ideally, the Conversions API through your store platform) fires a **Purchase** event with order value on the order confirmation page, and compare Meta-reported purchases with your store's orders.

## 5. Is there enough fresh creative?

Ads tire as the same people see them repeatedly. Watch frequency and click-through rate. When frequency climbs and results fall, refresh the creative rather than raising the budget. A steady supply of new photos, short videos and product shots keeps campaigns working. This is where performance marketing and [creative content](/creative) need to work together.

## The order to work in

1. Fix tracking, so results are measured in real orders.
2. Fix targeting and audiences, so spend reaches buyers.
3. Clean up captions and refresh creative.
4. Then increase budget on the campaigns that are profitable in your store's numbers, not just in Ads Manager.
