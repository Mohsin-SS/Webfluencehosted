Shopify, SEO & Paid Media

Latch sells everyday innerwear, loungewear and babywear for women, men and kids. Webfluence rebuilt the Shopify storefront from the ground up, then wired it to an SEO programme and Google and Meta campaigns, so the same team that built the landing pages runs the traffic that lands on them.

One growth loop

Paid media buys the first visit, the storefront turns it into an order, SEO lowers the cost of the next one.

01

Audience-first browsing

One storefront serves three audiences and a lot of SKUs. A single Women / Men / Baby switch sits at the top of the catalogue, and each audience gets illustrated category tiles, so a shopper picks "hipsters" or "co-ord sets" by sight rather than by reading a list. Tile grids reflow from five across on desktop to two on a phone.

Visit wearlatch.com
02

Product pages that answer first

A product like the Women's Bikini Brief ships as a pack of three in S to XL, so size, pack, price and the buy button need to sit together above the fold. The page leads with a size selector and size-chart link, a quantity stepper, and Add to cart and Buy it now stacked beneath. Descriptions and key features collapse underneath so the page stays short on mobile, and Buy it now goes straight to Shopify's checkout.

Visit wearlatch.com
03

A calm, campaign-led home

Full-bleed seasonal photography, the Latch wordmark centred, and a free-shipping bar that states the order threshold up front. Nothing competes with the product. Layouts are built mobile-first: single-column product pages and a hamburger-led header, with technical SEO (URLs, titles, descriptions, image weights, crawlable navigation) set up as part of development rather than retrofitted.

Request a Consultation
04

Making every collection page findable

Paid traffic stops when the budget stops, so the SEO programme exists to grow the share of orders that arrive without a bought click. Foundations first: crawlable navigation, canonical tags and sitemap submission. Then pages: titles, descriptions and copy for each collection and product written around the category term. A keyword map follows how innerwear is searched (product type, audience, pack size) with one page owning each cluster, and Search Console is reviewed on a schedule.

Request a Consultation
05

Paid traffic that lands on pages built to convert

Google Ads and Meta Ads are managed by the same team that built the store, so campaign structure follows the catalogue and every ad has a landing page designed for its intent. Ads map to specific collection and product pages, never only to the homepage, and budget moves weekly toward the campaigns and collections with the lowest cost per purchase.
